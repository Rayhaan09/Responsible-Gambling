Tips for Responsible Gambling

Smart Tips to Stay in Control

    Set a strict budget:
    Decide how much money you can afford to lose and never go beyond it.

    Never chase losses:
    Trying to win back money usually leads to losing more.

    Do not gamble when stressed or emotional:
    Gambling should never be used as an escape.

    Avoid borrowing money to gamble:
    If you can’t afford it, don’t risk it.

    Set time limits:
    Decide how long you will play before you start.

⚠️ Warning Signs of Gambling problem

  • Thinking about gambling all the time
  • Feeling anxious or angry when not gambling
  • Using gambling to escape problems
  • Spending more money than planned

Quick Self-Check

Ask yourself:

  • Am I gambling more than I can afford?
  • Have you tried to win back money you have lost

If you answered yes to any of these, it may be time to take a break and seek support.
