Facts & Statistics

Facts

• 1 in 5 gamblers experience financial difficulties.

• Problem gambling affects mental health, leading to stress, anxiety, and depression.

• Young adults are at higher risk, with 18-25 year-olds being the most affected age group.

• Gambling-related debt is a leading cause of bankruptcy among adults.

• Nearly 40% of problem gamblers also struggle with alcohol or substance abuse.

• Online gambling has increased accessibility, contributing to higher rates of addiction among teenagers and young adults.
